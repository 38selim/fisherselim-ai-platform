# FisherSelim AI

**FisherSelim AI** is a Turkish-first, multi-model AI chat platform built for users in Turkey.

🔗 **Website:** https://ai.fisherselim.com

## What it does

FisherSelim AI gives you access to multiple leading AI models — including **GPT, Claude, and Gemini** — in a single Turkish-language interface. It's designed specifically for Turkish users, with local payment support and Turkish-optimized responses.

## Features

- 🇹🇷 **Turkish-first:** Native Turkish interface and Turkish-optimized model responses
- 🤖 **Multi-model:** GPT, Claude, Gemini and more in one place
- 🎙️ **Voice cloning (SesKlon Deniz):** Integrated Turkish text-to-speech and voice cloning
- 💳 **Local payments:** İyzico integration for easy subscription with Turkish cards
- 💎 **Affordable plans:** Credit-based system with Premium and Business monthly tiers

## About

FisherSelim AI is developed by **FisherSelim Yapay Zeka Yazılım Teknolojileri** (Istanbul, Turkey).

## Türkçe

**FisherSelim AI**, Türkiye'deki kullanıcılar için geliştirilmiş, Türkçe öncelikli ve çok-modelli bir yapay zeka sohbet platformudur.
